# M5 – Simulation

The Simulation step replays the exact generated stroke plan from Strokes. A change to source, canvas, palette or brush settings invalidates that plan and requires regeneration. Workspace changes rebuild the service timeline while preserving source strokes.

Implemented: play/pause, restart, scrub timeline, 1×/10×/50×/200× speed, final-result preview, optional travel paths, current brush position and paint load, painting/travel distance, duration, load and wash counts. Canvas marks are clipped to the canvas area and incrementally cached; scrubbing backward rebuilds the raster deterministically.

The timeline translates canvas-local points into workspace coordinates. It schedules cup loads, wash/wipe on colour changes, mid-stroke reloads based on configured paint-load length, final cleanup and parking. Defaults are 1500 mm/min travel, 1.5 seconds load, 3 seconds wash and 2 seconds wipe. These are estimates pending hardware calibration.

Layout errors, missing cups and paths crossing forbidden zones (expanded by half the brush width) block playback. Automatic detour routing is not yet implemented. Clearance of all other equipment and machine Z motions still require export-stage validation. Stroke plans currently live in the session; reopened projects regenerate them in Strokes.

Verified with build and tests of deterministic timelines, capacity splitting and exact resumption, colour changes, timing, parking, forbidden crossings and interpolation. Export remains the next separate stage; service events are not yet wired into the GRBL postprocessor.
