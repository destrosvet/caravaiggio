# M5 – Simulation

The Simulation step replays the exact generated stroke plan from Strokes. A change to source, canvas, palette or brush settings invalidates that plan and requires regeneration. Workspace changes rebuild the service timeline while preserving source strokes.

Implemented: play/pause, restart, scrub timeline, 1×/10×/50×/200× speed, final-result preview, optional travel paths, current brush position and paint load, painting/travel distance, duration, load and wash counts. Canvas marks are clipped to the canvas area and incrementally cached; scrubbing backward rebuilds the raster deterministically.

The timeline translates canvas-local points into workspace coordinates. It schedules cup loads, wash/wipe on colour changes, mid-stroke reloads based on configured paint-load length, final cleanup and parking. Machine feeds, Z limits and editable XYZ service sequences now come from Workspace. Simulation replays the same explicit XYZ motions as Export, with a constant-speed estimate including vertical motion and service dwell. See [M6.md](M6.md).

Layout errors, missing cups and invalid machine/procedure settings block playback. Raised travel routes around forbidden rectangles expanded by half the brush width. Unreachable travel and forbidden painting/service motions block playback and export. Stroke plans currently live in the session; reopened projects regenerate them in Strokes.

Verified with build and tests of deterministic timelines, capacity splitting and exact resumption, colour changes, timing, parking, forbidden crossings and interpolation. The same machine program now drives GRBL XYZ export, including paint loading, washing and wiping.
